Bauble-Shake

Shake it till the baubles drop!

Bauble-Shake is a fast-paced multiplayer Christmas party game where everyone gets their own decorated Christmas tree. Shake your phone to knock off the decorations and race against your friends to clear your tree first!

A silly, competitive mobile party game that's perfect for Christmas gatherings, virtual parties and festive game nights.
